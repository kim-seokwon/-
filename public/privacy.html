Privacy Policy (English)

2179 Inc. — BHAS internal operations dashboard — Last updated 2026-10-06

1. About this service

BHAS is an internal, employees-only dashboard operated by 2179 Inc. for running its own children's apparel brands (HIHEYHO, LOHI STUDIO, TOVEE). It is not a consumer product and is not open to public sign-up. Access requires a company-issued account.

2. Data we process

  • Employee accounts: email, name, role — for authentication and access control.
  • Order data from our own online stores — for sales reporting and customer service.
  • Instagram data for accounts we own and operate: media, follower counts, like and comment counts.
  • Publicly available content (news articles, blog and forum posts): title, link, publication date.

3. Use of Meta Platform data

  • We retrieve media and metrics for Instagram Business accounts owned by our company and display them on internal reporting screens.
  • Where we query public hashtags or public business accounts, the sole purpose is internal market research for the children's apparel category.
  • Retrieved data is stored in Supabase (Seoul region) and is never sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. It is visible only to our employees.
  • We do not use the data to identify individuals, and we do not build user profiles.
  • We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

4. Retention

Public content records are deleted 180 days after collection. Order and accounting records are kept for the period required by Korean commerce law. Employee accounts are deleted when employment ends.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your data at any time. See Data Deletion for the deletion procedure.
